What the app is

Adopt Salmon Farm shows the locations of Norwegian salmon-aquaculture localities together with nearby vessel traffic (AIS), sourced from BarentsWatch open data (originating with the Norwegian Coastal Administration, Kystverket). It lets you set proximity alarms on farms and review recent vessel activity around them.

Informational use only — not a safety system

It is not a navigation, collision-avoidance, safety-of-life, security, surveillance, or law-enforcement system, and must not be relied upon as one. Vessel positions (AIS) and farm data can be delayed, incomplete, spoofed, or inaccurate, and alerts are best-effort and may be delayed or missed. Do not use Adopt Salmon Farm for emergency response or for any decision where a missed or wrong alert could cause harm. In an emergency, contact the appropriate authorities.

Subscriptions (Adopt Salmon Farm Live)

Exploring Adopt Salmon Farm is free: the map, vessel traffic, 24-hour vessel tracks, an alarm on one farm (up to a 500 m alert distance), and positions shown as snapshots that refresh approximately every five minutes.

The optional "Adopt Salmon Farm Live" auto-renewing subscription (US$2.99/month, or the local equivalent, billed to your Apple Account) adds continuous live position updates (about every 25 seconds), alarms on unlimited farms, an alert distance up to 2 km, per-farm visitor history (which vessels have passed a farm, how often, and when), weekly and monthly traffic trends, and fine-grained alert exclusions by vessel MMSI, IMO, flag, or size.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. You can manage or cancel the subscription, and turn off auto-renewal, in your Apple Account settings after purchase. The subscription price may change; where required, Apple will ask you to confirm a price increase before it takes effect, otherwise the then-current price applies on renewal. Any unused portion of a free trial, if offered, is forfeited when you buy a subscription.
